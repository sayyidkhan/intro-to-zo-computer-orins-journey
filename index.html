  3. 03Stricter enterprise requirements

My default Zo for the 0→1 journey.
Zo is not the answer to every infrastructure problem. Use it where learning velocity and time-to-outcome matter. Use a conventional VPS or a broader cloud platform when control, architecture, governance, or optimized scale is the primary constraint.
